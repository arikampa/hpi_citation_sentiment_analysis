FINAL robustness analysis: repeated 5-fold aggregated OOF GHPI.
Repeats=20; seeds=42-61.
For each citation and repeat, GHPI is estimated strictly out-of-fold.
The common threshold is the P75 of GHPI scores in that repeat's training folds only.
Per citation, repeated OOF GHPI scores and repeated OOF thresholds are aggregated by the median.
No seed is selected based on statistical significance.
The procedure is run on both mixed and real-only single-citation subsets.
All three sentiment classes are tested; BH correction is applied across the six architectures within each class.
Global_Gap aggregation=median.
